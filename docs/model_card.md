# Model Card: Repeat-Purchase Prediction

## Model Purpose

Predict whether a customer will make another purchase within 60 days after the analysis cutoff date.

The prediction is used to prioritize customers for retention and targeted marketing workflows.

## Intended Use

- Rank customers by repeat-purchase probability.
- Support marketing audience selection.
- Inform segment-level retention strategy.
- Provide inputs to the executive dashboard.

## Not Intended For

- Automated high-stakes decisions.
- Real customer targeting without further validation.
- Inferring causality from prediction scores alone.
- Replacing campaign holdout testing.

## Training Data

The training data is a simulated ecommerce customer dataset generated by `scripts/generate_data.py`.

Customer-level features are built from:

- Historical transactions.
- Customer attributes.
- Engagement and loyalty signals.
- Campaign response history.
- RFM-style behavior metrics.

## Target

`repeat_purchase_60d`: whether a customer purchased within 60 days after the cutoff date.

## Candidate Models

| Model | ROC AUC | Accuracy | Precision | Recall | F1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Logistic Regression | 0.697 | 0.636 | 0.538 | 0.592 | 0.564 |
| Random Forest | 0.709 | 0.647 | 0.560 | 0.525 | 0.542 |
| Gradient Boosting | 0.704 | 0.658 | 0.591 | 0.453 | 0.513 |

## Selected Model

Random Forest was selected because it had the strongest ROC AUC, which best matches the ranking use case.

## Key Features

- `orders_per_100_days`
- `monetary_per_100_days`
- `days_since_signup`
- `recency_days`
- `loyalty_score`
- `email_engagement_score`
- `discounted_revenue`

## Evaluation Notes

ROC AUC is the primary metric because the business needs a ranked customer list for targeting. Precision and recall are tracked because campaign costs and missed opportunities both matter.

## Risks And Limitations

- The data is simulated and should not be treated as production evidence.
- The model predicts likelihood to repeat purchase, not causal response to a campaign.
- Thresholds such as `Send targeted offer` should be calibrated with business costs.
- Segment behavior can drift over time, so monitoring would be required in production.

## Recommended Monitoring

- Prediction distribution drift.
- Segment mix drift.
- Feature drift for recency, frequency, monetary value, and engagement.
- Actual repeat-purchase rate by score decile.
- Campaign ROI by model score band.
